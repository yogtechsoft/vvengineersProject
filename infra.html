Our Manufacturing Infrastructure

At V V Engineers, we are equipped with modern fabrication machinery and industrial equipment to ensure precision manufacturing, high productivity, quality workmanship, and timely project execution.

Our facility is capable of handling a wide range of fabrication, welding, cutting, rolling, bending, grinding, and material handling operations for industrial process equipment and heavy fabrication projects.

Machinery & Equipment

Welding Machines

We are equipped with multiple heavy-duty welding machines suitable for industrial fabrication and structural welding applications.

  • Tig Welding Machines – 400 AMP
  • ARC Welding Machines – 400 AMP

These machines support high-quality welding for Stainless Steel and Mild Steel fabrication works.

Plasma Cutting Machine

Our plasma cutting machine enables accurate and efficient metal cutting for various fabrication requirements

  • Weld Arc Plasma Machine – 400 AMP

The machine ensures precision cutting with improved productivity and clean finishing

Plate Rolling Machine

We have a heavy-duty plate rolling machine capable of handling large fabrication requirements.

  • Plate Rolling Capacity: Up to 1800 mm Width
  • Thickness Capacity: Up to 12 mm

This machine is used for plate rolling.

Pipe Bending Machines

Our pipe bending machines support accurate pipe forming and fabrication activities.Available capacities include:

  • 25 NB to 80 NB

These machines are suitable for industrial piping and process equipment fabrication.

Drilling Machine

We have industrial drilling equipment for accurate hole-making and fabrication support operations.

  • National Engineering Drilling Machine
  • 40 mm Capacity

Suitable for heavy fabrication and structural works.

Hydraulic Pump

We are equipped with hydraulic systems for heavy-duty industrial operations.

  • JP Kissan Hydraulic Pump – 1440 RPM

Used for supporting fabrication and hydraulic applications.

Material Handling Equipment

EOT Cranes

Our workshop is equipped with material handling cranes for safe and efficient movement of heavy fabrication components.

  • Crane 01 – 5 Ton Capacity
  • Crane 02 – 5 Ton Capacity

These cranes support heavy equipment fabrication and assembly operations.

Air Compressor

We use industrial air compressors for pneumatic tools and workshop operations.

  • Modern Air Compressor – 5 HP

Supports fabrication, cleaning, and pneumatic applications throughout the facility.

Manufacturing Capabilities

  • Plate Rolling & Pipe Bending
  • Structural Fabrication
  • Custom Engineering Solutions

Our infrastructure enables us to deliver quality-focused fabrication solutions for various industrial sectors with reliability, precision, and timely execution.
